# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-09-09
First versioned release. The app as it stands today:
- Load any RCSB PDB ID; view as Cartoon, Ribbon, Tube, Trace, Stick, Ball & Stick, Sphere, or Surface
- Color by chain, secondary structure, element, residue type, B-factor, rainbow, or hydrophobicity
- Per-chain and per-residue selection, including drag-to-select/deselect across residues
- Black-and-white "Sketch style" rendering, with adjustable line thickness, shades of gray, and tint color
- Export PNG and Export OBJ (real geometry, not billboard impostors)
- Smoothed Surface representation (reduced marching-cubes spike artifacts)
- Version number shown in the footer (this file)
